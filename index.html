OUR SERVICES

From jersey customization to full brand identity — every creative need, one studio.

01 / Service

JERSEY SELLING

We stock a premium selection of football jerseys from top clubs and national teams. Whether you want the latest EPL kit, an African champions replica, or a rare vintage edition — we source quality you can trust.

  • Authentic quality replica jerseys from global clubs
  • African, European and international national team kits
  • Vintage and retro editions for collectors
  • Bulk orders for schools, companies and events
02 / Service

JERSEY CUSTOMIZATION

Bring your own jersey or buy from us — we'll transform it into something uniquely yours. State-of-the-art printing technology ensures your customization is sharp, durable, and professional.

  • Player names and numbers with heat transfer & vinyl
  • Patch and badge application (sleeve, chest, back)
  • Custom sponsor logos and branding
  • Full team kit customization for clubs and corporates
03 / Service

BUSINESS BRANDING

Your brand is your identity. We craft logos that speak volumes, websites that convert, and brand systems that create lasting impressions in a crowded market.

  • ▸ Professional logo design and brand identity packages
  • ▸ Website design and development for businesses
  • ▸ Social media branding kits and templates
  • ▸ Business cards, letterheads, and stationery design
04 / Service

MERCHANDISE PRINTING

Turn your logo or design into a merchandise empire. From hoodies to mugs, caps to tote bags — we print quality merch that keeps your brand front and center.

  • Hoodies, t-shirts and caps with custom logo printing
  • Branded mugs, keyrings and promotional items
  • Event merchandise for concerts, launches and sports
  • Corporate branded gifts and uniform packages

Who We Are

CREATIVITY
MEETS PURPOSE

Brandplus Studio was born out of a simple but powerful idea: that creativity should be accessible, purposeful, and fearless. Founded along the Catholic-Kambi ya Juu Highway in Kula Mawe, we started as a small jersey shop and grew into a full-service creative studio.

We believe football isn't just a sport — it's a culture, a language, and a canvas. We've taken that energy and applied it to every service we offer: branding that's bold, websites that convert, merchandise that turns heads, and jerseys that make you feel like a champion.

  • Football culture + professional design = Brandplus DNA
  • We work with local businesses, teams, schools and corporates
  • Every project gets the same passion — big or small
  • Based in Kenya, thinking globally
